Privacy Policy — CCE Software

Effective date: 22 July 2026  |  Last updated: 27 July 2026

CCE Software ("the App", "we", "us") is a school-administration application used by schools to record and manage Continuous & Comprehensive Evaluation (CCE) data — student marks, attendance, and related academic records — for students studying under the Maharashtra CCE framework. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices available to schools, teachers, and administrators who use it.

This App is published and maintained by Sugave Godavari, contactable at sugavegodavari476@gmail.com.

By creating an account or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Policy.

1. Who Uses This App

CCE Software is intended for use by schools, teachers, and school administrators — not directly by students or children. Any student information present in the App (names, class, marks, attendance, photographs) is entered and managed by authorised school staff on behalf of the school, as part of the school's own academic record-keeping.

The App does not collect precise location, contacts, camera/microphone access, or health data. We do not collect or store full card numbers, bank account numbers, or UPI PINs — only a transaction reference and amount for verification purposes.

3. How We Use Information

  • To create and secure school/teacher accounts and authenticate logins
  • To store and organise each school's academic records (marks, attendance, evaluations)
  • To synchronise data across the devices used by the same school, so multiple staff can work on the same records
  • To detect and block suspicious or repeated failed login attempts (fraud/abuse prevention)
  • To generate printable reports, report cards, and ID cards on behalf of the school
  • To verify UPI/bank transfer payments and activate the correct license tier (Standard, Pro, or Premium) for a school

We do not use this data for advertising, and we do not sell any data to third parties.

4. How Data Is Stored

The App works offline-first: all data is saved on the device itself first (local storage), and is then synced in the background to Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore, scoped separately per school, so that one school's data cannot be read by another school.

Firebase (a Google Cloud service) acts as our data processor and stores data on our behalf, secured with access rules that isolate each school's records. Data in transit between the App and Firebase is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).

Firebase infrastructure may store data on servers located outside India as part of Google's global cloud infrastructure. By using the App, you consent to this processing.

5. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or rent personal or student data to advertisers or unrelated third parties. Data is only accessible to:

  • The school's own authorised staff (teachers/admins) who are given login access by the school
  • Google Firebase, solely as our cloud storage/infrastructure provider, under Google's own data processing and security terms
  • Government education authorities, only if the school itself chooses to export/share a report for official/statutory reporting purposes

6. Children's Information

The App is not directed at children and is not used directly by children. Student records (including names, marks, and photographs) are entered and controlled entirely by the school as part of its own academic administration, in the same way a school maintains paper registers.

Schools are responsible for obtaining any parental/institutional consent required under applicable local law before entering student information into any digital record-keeping system, including this App.

7. Data Retention

Academic records are retained for as long as the school's account remains active, or as required by the school's own record-keeping obligations. Payment transaction references are retained only as long as necessary to verify the license and resolve any billing disputes.

A school administrator may request deletion of their school's account and associated data at any time (see Section 9). Once a deletion request is processed, data is permanently removed from our active systems; any residual copies in routine backups are purged within 30 days.

8. Security

  • Passwords are never stored in plain text — only a salted cryptographic hash is stored
  • Each school's data is isolated from other schools
  • Repeated failed login attempts are automatically detected and locked out
  • Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS)
  • No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information in our care
